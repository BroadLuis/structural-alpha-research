Weekly Market Review: Equities Recover as Gold and Silver Miners Remain Under Pressure

Published November 14, 2026 · Data through November 13, 2026 · 17 monitored universes · Structural Alpha Research

Global equities ended the week with stronger price participation and, in several markets, improving 200-day moving-average breadth. Precious-metals miners, juniors and much of the materials complex remained under significant pressure. The central contrast: the semiconductor basket gained 9.46% at the weekly median, yet just 30.30% of its stocks remained above MA200; silver miners lost 5.95% at the median, with MA200 breadth at only 9.28%.

2. Gold and silver miners: weakness remains broad

Gold miners (GDX–GDXJ)

The 146-instrument gold-mining universe lost 6.98% at the weekly median. MA200 breadth reached 20.00%, down from 29.66% four weeks earlier, while the median distance to MA200 measured −13.60%. The median 52-week drawdown was −36.15%, and median signed-volume balance remained negative at −13.62. Of the calibrated observations, 43.45% were below their respective historical P10 thresholds for distance to MA200.

Silver miners (SIL–SILJ)

Silver-mining-related instruments recorded a median weekly decline of 5.95%. Just 9.28% remained above MA200, versus 26.80% four weeks earlier. Median distance to MA200 was −20.31% and median 52-week drawdown −43.16%. Median signed volume measured −21.50; 52.58% of calibrated instruments fell below their own historical P10 distance thresholds.

Interpretation: prices, breadth and volume all depict broad weakness. Extreme readings are descriptive evidence of strain; they do not, by themselves, identify a market bottom, establish capitulation or constitute a validated entry signal.

3. Junior miners, copper and materials: structural damage persists

The JUNIOR universe fell 5.49% at the weekly median, with only 13.22% above MA200, a median 52-week drawdown of −50.53% and median distance to MA200 of −24.37%. Junior copper was similarly impaired: −4.36% weekly, 13.11% above MA200 and −50.59% median drawdown.

Broad COPPER was less negative over the week (−0.50% median), but MA200 breadth was just 18.00%, down from 37.00% four weeks earlier. The median signed-volume balance remained negative (−22.56).

Additional materials baskets underline the dispersion of damage. URANIUM had 12.33% above MA200 and a −52.86% median drawdown; PLATINUM had 5.00% breadth and −55.00% median drawdown. LITHIUM registered 0.00% above MA200, with a median distance of −41.62% and median 52-week drawdown of −66.84%.

4. Semiconductor rebound: price recovery is not trend repair

Semiconductors delivered a powerful +9.46% median weekly gain. Nonetheless, only 30.30% of instruments traded above MA200, compared with 42.42% four weeks earlier. Median distance to MA200 remained negative at −8.26%; the median 52-week drawdown was −32.82%. Median signed-volume balance was only marginally positive at +0.30.

Interpretation: the rebound was meaningful in price terms, but current breadth is insufficient to describe a fully restored medium-term market structure.

5. Large-cap equities and US banks show stronger participation

The S&P 500 posted a +2.19% median weekly return with 62.80% above MA200, up 11.60 percentage points over four weeks. The STOXX 600 gained +2.02% at the median; its MA200 breadth reached 69.19%, up 10.07 points, with a positive median signed-volume balance of +12.34.

The NASDAQ 100 advanced +2.90% at the weekly median, with 55.56% above MA200. Unlike the S&P 500 and STOXX 600, its comparable four-week breadth showed no improvement.

US BANKS stood out for the 25.68-percentage-point four-week increase in MA200 breadth, from 43.24% to 68.92%. Median signed volume was positive at +9.61, and its median 52-week drawdown was comparatively modest at −9.21%.

6. Energy, ASX and agribusiness: mixed recovery signals

ENERGY rebounded +5.45% at the weekly median, yet MA200 breadth had declined from 62.05% to 43.59% over four weeks; median signed volume was −0.29. The price rebound therefore did not erase the preceding breadth deterioration.

ASX miners gained +1.10% at the median, but just 30.34% remained above MA200, with a −38.36% median drawdown. AGRIBUSINESS gained +0.67%, with 30.43% breadth, while FERTILIZERS was broadly flat at +0.03% with 29.41% breadth. These are partial improvements rather than evidence of uniformly restored trends.

7. Observations, interpretation and conditional scenarios

Measured observations. Broad equity markets and US banks currently show stronger MA200 participation and generally healthier signed-volume balances than precious-metals miners and several materials baskets. Semiconductors and energy enjoyed strong weekly gains without equivalent medium-term breadth repair.

Interpretation. The data show substantial cross-sector divergence. A strong weekly return and a resilient longer-term market structure are distinct concepts. MA200 breadth is also a lagging descriptor, not a standalone forecast.

Conditional scenarios. A more convincing mining recovery would involve sustained expansion of MA200 breadth, improving volume participation and narrowing drawdowns. Rebounds with persistently low breadth would be compatible with a prolonged consolidation. Continued negative breadth and volume, meanwhile, would leave the risk of further deterioration open. These are conditions to monitor, not probability forecasts.
