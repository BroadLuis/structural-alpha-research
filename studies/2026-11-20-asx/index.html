Weekly research on 89 Australian-listed mining stocks: record-low market breadth, widespread drawdowns, selling pressure, and the complete company ranking.

Executive summary. Australian-listed mining stocks recorded their worst average weekly return since our historical series began in December 2023. Across the selected 89-company universe for November 13–20, 2026, deteriorating breadth, deepening drawdowns, and increased selling pressure point to widespread liquidation. These readings are consistent with capitulation, but do not establish a market bottom.

1. Weekly performance and market breadth

Only three of 89 companies advanced, two were unchanged, and 84 declined. Eighty fell at least 5%, 66 fell at least 10%, and eight fell at least 20%. Even the 25 best weekly performers lost an average of 3.89%, while the weakest 25 lost 19.50%.

7. Conclusion: extreme weakness is not a confirmed bottom

Measured: the universe is experiencing one of the most extreme liquidation phases in the available history, with record-low MA200 participation, widespread drawdowns, and increased selling pressure.

Interpretation: the combination is consistent with capitulation. However, extreme oversold conditions are not sufficient to confirm a bottom.

What to watch: stabilization in market breadth, a reduction in selling pressure, and positive divergences in the market's internal behavior. We do not need every company to begin rising simultaneously. The important development would be identifying which stocks start showing resilience while the broader market is still falling.

8. Methodology and limitations

This descriptive analysis uses an equal-weighted selected universe of 89 Australian-listed mining securities, not the entire ASX market. Publication and observation date: November 20, 2026; weekly comparison: November 13–20. MA200 is the 200-session moving average; breadth uses 89 valid observations. Drawdowns measure the latest close relative to the trailing 52-week closing high (88 valid observations). Relative volume compares the most recent five sessions with the preceding 60-session reference; signed volume measures price-direction-weighted volume over 20 sessions, not institutional fund flows. The historical series contains 156 weekly observations of the tracked universe, with changing availability; it is not a point-in-time, survivorship-free reconstruction. Five stocks lack a valid global rank, and Titan Minerals was excluded because its last recorded quote was October 30. Vault Minerals and Mindax have latest quotes dated November 17 and 19 respectively. Weekly and global ranks measure different horizons and do not constitute investment recommendations. See the public methodology and limitations.
