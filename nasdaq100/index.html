Nasdaq 100

Independent quantitative research into the market structure of the Nasdaq 100: breadth, long-term trends, relative strength, drawdowns and volume participation. We look beyond headline index returns to examine what is happening across the monitored securities.

Understanding the universe

The Nasdaq 100 is a modified market-capitalization-weighted index of major non-financial companies listed on the Nasdaq Stock Market. It is heavily exposed to technology, but also includes businesses from other sectors. Index returns and equal-weight measurements of its constituents can tell different stories.

Our weekly research evaluates company-level participation alongside the index benchmark. Coverage can include multiple share classes and may differ from official constituent counts at a given date; each dated study identifies its actual sample and eligible observations.

How to interpret our findings

A weak breadth reading or an extreme historical percentile describes the condition of the market, not a confirmed turning point. Our research separates observed data from interpretation and scenarios. Historical distributions are contextual evidence rather than guarantees of future performance.
