A public library of data-driven studies of market structure, breadth, relative strength, drawdowns, and trading volume. Research documents and supporting files will be available to read and download free of charge.

Our approach

We study what is happening beneath index and sector prices: how many securities are participating, where strength and weakness are concentrated, and whether price movements are supported by volume. Conclusions are based on observed data; scenarios are distinguished from confirmed signals.
