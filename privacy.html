Privacy & Cookies Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Structural Alpha Research is a free, independent market research website. No account, subscription or payment is required to read studies or download published materials.

Information and technologies used

The site uses browser local storage to remember appearance preferences (light or dark theme). This is a functional preference, not an advertising profile. You can clear it through your browser settings.

Pages may request resources from hits.sh, a third-party page-view counter. According to the provider's published privacy policy, the hit-counter service does not track IP addresses, use cookies, or collect user-agent strings. It processes only the count associated with the badge URL. This does not describe visits to the hits.sh website itself. See hits.sh privacy policy. Counts displayed on this site are approximate and do not represent verified unique visitors.

Hosting through GitHub Pages also entails technical requests to the hosting infrastructure. The hosting provider may process standard connection and security logs under its own policies.

Cookies and consent

We do not intentionally use advertising cookies or Google Analytics in the pages reviewed. The theme preference is stored locally. This assessment reflects the reviewed site code and the provider's published policy. Third-party practices may change. If non-essential cookies or comparable tracking requiring consent are introduced, the site must request appropriate consent before activating them.

External links and downloads

Some studies link to third-party sites or downloadable Excel workbooks. Following external links is subject to those services' privacy practices. Downloading a file does not require creating an account here.

Your choices

You can remove local storage through your browser's site-data controls and use browser privacy protections to limit third-party requests. For privacy inquiries or correction requests, please contact the project owner through the project repository. Avoid posting sensitive personal information in public issues.

Changes

This notice will be updated if the site's features, analytics or external services materially change.
