Understanding the universe

Our SEMICONDUCTORS research universe monitors 33 listed businesses spanning chip design, manufacturing, foundries, semiconductor equipment and related technology. It is a curated research basket, not a replication of the PHLX Semiconductor Index.

The benchmark is the PHLX Semiconductor Index (.SOX), which is excluded from the 33-company rankings. Equal-weight breadth and median security returns can differ materially from benchmark index returns. Our historical review contains 155 consecutive weekly observations through November 6, 2026.

How to interpret our findings

A weak breadth reading or an extreme historical percentile describes the condition of the market, not a confirmed turning point. Our research separates observed data from interpretation and scenarios. Historical distributions are contextual evidence rather than guarantees of future performance.
