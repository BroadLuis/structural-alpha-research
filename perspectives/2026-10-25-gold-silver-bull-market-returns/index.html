Gold and Silver Mining Returns Since February 2024: A 203-Security Drawdown Study

Despite severe pullbacks from their peaks, most securities in the examined gold-and-silver-focused universe remain above their prices at the beginning of the current bull-market reference period.

From the February 29, 2024 reference date to late October 2026, 186 of 203 securities (91.6%) gained, while 17 (8.4%) declined. The median cumulative price return is +209.67% — even though median current drawdown from the highest recorded close within the study period stands at −38.82%.

The distinction: a 40% retreat from a sharply elevated peak does not mean a security has lost 40% relative to the beginning of the bull market. The starting point matters.

1. Bull-market gains have largely survived the correction

The distribution is heavily skewed toward positive cumulative performance. The median security has more than tripled its price relative to its first available closing observation on or just after February 29, 2024. The study describes observed price outcomes, not an investable portfolio return or an equally weighted index.

91.6% positive

Some examples at the top of the ranked universe are Benz Mining (+2,774%), Omai Gold Mines (+1,911%) and Montage Gold (+1,785%). These substantial returns should not be confused with expectations of future performance.

2. Drawdowns tell a different, equally important story

The median maximum drawdown is −50.22%; the median current drawdown is −38.82%. Maximum drawdown measures the largest peak-to-subsequent-trough decline observed within the study window. Current drawdown measures the decline from the study-period closing high to the latest available close. The two need not occur during the same market selloff.

This helps explain why sentiment can be deeply negative even when cumulative returns over the entire cycle remain substantial. Sharp corrections and sizeable gains measured from an earlier baseline can coexist.

3. Which securities returned to or below their starting prices?

Only 17 securities in the sample show negative price returns since their starting observation. Their weakness deserves company-specific scrutiny: financial condition, dilution, project execution, corporate actions and whether the selling exceeded what changed fundamentally. A steep fall alone does not imply undervaluation or a buying opportunity.

Other securities have retained considerable gains while falling steeply from interim highs, which makes both the cumulative return and the drawdown necessary for a fair comparison.

4. Interpretation: correction severity is not trend termination

Observed: 91.6% of the sampled securities remain above the study's initial reference prices, notwithstanding deep drawdowns. Interpretation: this pattern is consistent with a powerful advance followed by a severe correction, rather than the complete reversal of gains across the sample. Not established: whether a market bottom is in, whether every issuer's underlying trend remains bullish, or whether subsequent returns will be favorable.

The evidence supports separating how much has been given back from a peak from how much was gained since the cycle began. Neither measure should be interpreted in isolation.

Methodology and limitations

The workbook defines a nominal starting date of February 29, 2024. Certain securities have their earliest eligible close on March 1 or March 4, 2024. The latest available close is dated October 22 or 23, 2026, depending on the instrument. Company rows preserve the actual start/end dates, closing-price levels and percentage values supplied in the original spreadsheet.

Return is cumulative price change between those observations. Maximum drawdown is the worst observed peak-to-trough percentage decline during the interval. Current drawdown measures the latest close relative to the interval's highest close. These are price-based measures, not total returns adjusted for dividends.

The source workbook contains a selected group of 203 mining-linked securities, including some ETFs and instruments outside pure-play gold and silver miners. Results must not be extrapolated to every listed miner or treated as the return of a tradable index. The selected surviving universe may suffer selection, survivorship, corporate-action, adjusted-price and listing-history effects; local price currencies vary. No fundamental assessment of the 17 negative-return names has been performed.

This study is descriptive research, not a prediction, trading signal or personalized recommendation. See the public methodology guide.
